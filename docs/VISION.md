# SuiPump — Complete Product Vision & Design

---

## The Core Philosophy

SuiPump is not a pump.fun clone. It is a token lifecycle operating system built on SUI. The distinction matters because it defines every product decision.

Pump.fun solved one problem well: make token creation trivially easy and give it a built-in market. But it stops there. The moment a token graduates, users scatter — to Raydium for trading, to Discord for community, to Dexscreener for charts, to Birdeye for analytics, to separate Telegram bots for alerts. The platform's relationship with the token ends at graduation.

SuiPump's relationship with a token never ends. From the moment a creator uploads a meme to the moment whales are trading it on Cetus with deep liquidity, everything happens within one ecosystem. That is the vision.

---

## Who We Are Building For

There are five distinct users and each needs to feel the platform was built specifically for them.

The Degen Trader — moves fast, has multiple tabs open, thinks in percentages and curve progress bars. Needs sub-second price updates, one-click buys, and a reason to stay on the platform after graduation instead of going to Cetus directly. Their loyalty is earned by giving them information edges they can't get elsewhere.

The Meme Creator — usually not technical. May have never used a crypto wallet before. Has an audience somewhere (Twitter, Telegram, a group chat) and wants to turn that attention into a token. Needs zero-friction creation, a beautiful shareable page for their token, and a direct financial relationship with their community's trading activity.

The Developer / Bot Builder — building a sniping bot, a copy-trade tool, a portfolio tracker, or a Telegram alert bot. Needs a clean API, predictable WebSocket events, typed responses, and the ability to submit atomic multi-token operations. Treats the platform as infrastructure, not as a UI.

The Community Member — follows a creator, holds tokens as identity and membership, wants to access exclusive content, be part of the conversation, and feel like holding the token means something beyond price speculation.

The Institutional Trader / Market Maker — arrives post-graduation, needs limit orders, spread control, and real liquidity mechanics. Currently has no reason to touch a meme launchpad. Future DeepBook integration (post-mainnet) would give them one.

## The Token Lifecycle — Stage by Stage

Every product decision maps to a stage in this lifecycle. Nothing is built without a clear answer to "which stage does this serve and for which user?"

### Stage 1: Discovery (before creation)

The creator has an idea. They land on the platform and immediately understand: this is where SUI memes are born. The discovery page is the heartbeat of the platform — a live feed of what's being created, what's pumping, what's about to graduate. It communicates energy and activity.

The King of the Hill slot sits at the top — one token, the one with the highest trading volume in the last hour. It rotates. Communities compete for it. This single mechanic creates urgency and tribal competition that the pump.fun community discovered naturally — we design it intentionally.

Below it: a trending section (volume-weighted, not just recent), a new tokens section (chronological, last 30 minutes), and an almost graduating section (tokens above 80% curve completion). That last section is particularly powerful — it creates a natural call to action for traders who want to ride the graduation pump.

Search works by name, symbol, or creator handle. Filters by graduation status, age, volume, holder count.

### Stage 2: Creation

This is where SUI's technical advantages turn into UX advantages.

A first-time user arrives from a Twitter link. No wallet. No SUI. They click "Create Token" and are offered two paths: connect a wallet (for crypto-native users) or sign in with Google (zkLogin). The Google path generates a real SUI wallet silently, behind the scenes. From their perspective they just signed in with Google.
They fill in: name, ticker (max 10 chars), description, and upload their meme. The meme can be a static image, an animated GIF, or a short video — all stored permanently on Walrus. This is a meaningful differentiator. On pump.fun your meme is an IPFS hash that can vanish. On SuiPump your meme is on-chain forever.

They set optional social links: Twitter, Telegram, website. They preview what their token page will look like before deploying.

Fee is 0.5 SUI. Gas is sponsored for new users (Enoki). Creation uses a 2-click OTW flow: (1) user signs to publish a minimal OTW package (generated by the backend from the token name/symbol), (2) user signs to create the bonding curve, register the token, initialize the creator vault, and reference the media blob. Two clicks, token is live.

The moment the transaction confirms, the creator gets a shareable link with their token's page. That link is the product they share on Twitter. The page has to be beautiful enough that sharing it feels good.

### Stage 3: The Bonding Curve Phase

This is where most user time is spent. The bonding curve page needs to communicate three things simultaneously: price momentum, community activity, and urgency.

Price momentum is communicated through a TradingView-style candlestick chart built from on-chain trade events. Every buy and sell is a data point. The chart updates in real time via WebSocket. There is no polling. The moment a trade confirms on-chain, the chart moves.

Below the chart: the bonding curve progress bar. Not a percentage number — a visual bar that fills up as SUI pours in. When it hits 100%, graduation triggers. This bar is the most emotionally engaging element on the page. Watching it move in real time while a community is aping in is viscerally exciting. We make it prominent.

Community activity lives in the trade feed on the right side of the page. Every buy and sell, in real time, with wallet address (or SuiNS name), amount, and whether they're buying or selling. Large buys get visual emphasis — a whale alert styling. This feed is the social proof engine. Seeing wallets pile in triggers FOMO. Seeing sells triggers analysis. It's the page's pulse.

Token-gated holder chat sits below or beside the trade feed. If you hold the token, you see the chat. If you don't, you see a blurred preview and the message "Hold 1,000 DOGE2 to join the conversation." This is powered by Seal's threshold encryption. No Discord bot. No off-chain verification. The access control is the smart contract. This keeps the community on the platform.

Creator gated content is a section visible only to holders above a configurable threshold. The creator uploads this content during or after creation: alpha posts, voice notes, exclusive memes, private Telegram invite links, early access codes. Walrus stores the encrypted media. Seal enforces access. The creator sets the minimum token balance required to decrypt.

This mechanic fundamentally changes what it means to hold a meme token. It's not just speculation. It's membership.

The buy/sell widget is pinned in a clean sidebar. SUI amount input, expected tokens output (updates as you type, same formula as the contract), price impact warning if above 5%, slippage setting, and a prominent buy button. Sell is a tab switch away. One-click max buttons. No confirmation dialogs unless price impact is extreme.

Holder information below the chart: total holders, top 10 holders as a bar chart (showing concentration), and a flag if the creator/dev wallet has sold more than a threshold percentage. Transparency about concentration is a trust feature.

Creator profile card shows: their SuiNS name if set, how many tokens they've created, their graduation rate, and their reputation score. This contextualizes whether this creator has a track record or is unknown.

### Stage 4: Graduation

Graduation is a moment. It should feel like an event, not a background process.
When the curve hits 100%, a visual graduation animation plays on the token page. The progress bar completes. A countdown appears: "Graduating in ~5 seconds." The platform's graduation watcher detects the threshold on-chain, builds and submits the graduation PTB, and the transaction creates a Cetus CLMM pool.

The LP is burned immediately. The burn transaction hash is displayed on the token page permanently: "Liquidity locked forever — burned at tx 0x..." This is a trust signal. Users can verify it. It means the creator cannot rug post-graduation.

Graduation is also announced across the platform: a banner on the discovery page, a Telegram bot notification, a WebSocket event that all connected clients receive. Bot builders subscribe to graduation events because they're trading signals.

Post-graduation, the token page transforms. The bonding curve chart becomes a Cetus price chart. The buy/sell widget deep-links to Cetus for AMM trading. The holder-only chat and gated content remain active — the community doesn't disappear just because the curve completed.

### Stage 5: Post-Graduation Ecosystem

This is where pump.fun stops and SuiPump continues.

A graduated token on SuiPump has:
- Permanent token page with full trade history and analytics
- Cetus AMM pool with locked LP (verifiable burn)
- Optional DeepBook CLOB for limit orders (post-mainnet future enhancement)
- Ongoing creator fee from Cetus LP fees (creator gets share of LP earnings)
- Holder-only chat remains active (you still hold the token)
- Gated content remains accessible (based on current balance)
- Deep-link to Bluefin for perpetuals if enough liquidity develops

The post-graduation page becomes a reference page for the token. It's what someone shares when they're evangelizing a token that's already made it.

## The API Layer — Design Philosophy

The API is the platform's second product. It has its own users (developers), its own documentation, its own SDK. It is not an afterthought bolted onto the frontend.

### The key design decision: unsigned PTBs

Every trade endpoint returns serialized, unsigned PTB bytes. The developer signs them with their own keypair and submits to the SUI network directly. The platform never touches private keys. This is critical for bot builders — they need to trust that the platform cannot front-run, cannot intercept, cannot manipulate their trades.

This matches how Jupiter works on Solana. Jupiter builds the swap transaction, you sign it, you submit it. That trust model is why the Solana bot ecosystem built on top of Jupiter. We replicate it.

### The three tiers of developer

Free tier — 60 requests per minute. Access to all GET endpoints. Can build read-only analytics tools, portfolio trackers, price bots. Cannot access agent endpoints (batch buy, copy-trade webhooks).

Pro tier — 600 requests per minute. Access to all PTB-building endpoints. Can build single-token trading bots, alert systems, custom frontends.

Agent tier — 6,000 requests per minute, priority WebSocket connection, access to batch-buy PTB endpoint, copy-trade subscriptions, webhook delivery. Built for serious automated trading systems.

### The WebSocket design

Four channel types: new_tokens (fires on every token creation, within 200ms of on-chain confirmation), trades (fires on every buy/sell, can filter by coin type), graduation (fires when any token graduates), wallet_trades (fires when a specific wallet makes any trade — the copy-trade signal).

The WebSocket connection auto-reconnects. Clients send a ping every 30 seconds. If no pong within 5 seconds, reconnect. Events are idempotent — each has a unique event ID derived from the transaction hash and event sequence, so clients can deduplicate safely.

### The batch-buy endpoint — why it matters
A typical sniping bot on Solana has to submit N sequential transactions to buy N tokens. If the first succeeds but the second fails (because someone else bought ahead of it), the bot has a partial position it didn't want. Managing this is complex and error-prone.

On SuiPump, the batch-buy endpoint builds a single PTB containing up to 10 sequential move calls. When the bot submits this, either all 10 buys execute or none do. The atomicity is enforced at the protocol level, not by the bot's retry logic. This is a fundamental capability difference that serious bot builders will pay the Agent tier for.

### The copy-trade webhook

A developer subscribes to a target wallet. Every time that wallet buys or sells on SuiPump, the platform fires a webhook to the developer's endpoint. The webhook payload includes the trade details AND a pre-built unsigned PTB for the same trade (scaled by the developer's configured ratio). The developer's bot validates the payload (HMAC signature), optionally applies its own filters, and submits the PTB. Near-zero additional latency between target trade and follower trade.

## Identity and Reputation

SuiNS integration means creators have human-readable identities. @whale.sui created 8 tokens, 3 of which graduated. That's information. That's reputation. That's the basis of trust before aping into a new token.

The creator reputation score is calculated on-chain from indexable data: total tokens launched, graduation rate percentage, average holder ROI across all tokens, whether the creator's wallet sold a significant percentage within 24 hours of launch. It is a public score, visible on every token page that creator makes, visible on their profile. It cannot be gamed easily because it's derived from on-chain history.

A creator with a high reputation score will see their new tokens trend faster — not through algorithmic boost, but because experienced traders filter by creator reputation before buying. This creates a virtuous cycle: good creators build reputation, reputation attracts earlier buyers, earlier buyers enable faster graduation, graduation strengthens reputation.

The profile page at /profile/@handle.sui shows all tokens created, their current status, total volume generated, total creator fees earned (lifetime), reputation score breakdown, and any gated content collections they've published.

## The Mobile Experience

A significant portion of meme token trading happens on mobile. The platform must be fully functional on mobile, not a degraded experience.

The discovery feed is a vertical scroll of token cards — optimized for thumb scrolling. Each card shows the token image (animated if GIF), name, symbol, price, 24h change, and curve progress bar. Tapping opens the token page.

The token page on mobile collapses the chart above the fold, with the buy widget pinned to the bottom of the screen as a persistent action bar. Swipe up to see the full chart, trade feed, and holder chat.

zkLogin means mobile users don't need MetaMask mobile or a wallet app. They sign in with Google on mobile Safari or Chrome, get a wallet, and trade. This is the onboarding flow that captures users who would never download a crypto wallet but will click a Twitter link.

## The Telegram Bot

The official SuiPump Telegram bot is a platform feature, not an external product. It covers:

Alerts you set from the website: notifications when a token you hold reaches a price target, when curve progress crosses 90%, when a token you're watching graduates, when the creator sells.

New token feed: a channel that posts every new token launch with image, name, creator reputation score, and a direct link to the token page.

Whale alerts: a channel that posts any single buy above a configurable SUI threshold.

Graduation announcements: a channel that posts every graduation with the Cetus pool link and the LP burn transaction.
The bot's read-only data comes from the WebSocket API. It is effectively a WebSocket client with Telegram message formatting. Third-party developers can build their own bots using the same API — the official bot is proof of what's possible and a reference implementation.

## Trust and Safety

Every meme launchpad has rug pulls. The question is not whether they happen — they will — but whether the platform gives users the information to protect themselves and the mechanics to limit damage.

Creator wallet tracking: the indexer tracks the creator's wallet address (from the creation transaction). If that address sells more than 40% of their holdings within 24 hours of launch, the token page shows a visible warning: "Dev sold X% of holdings." This is not a block. It is information. Users can make their own choices.

Concentration risk display: the holder distribution chart shows if the top 3 wallets hold more than 50% of supply. High concentration is a risk signal displayed prominently, not hidden.

Graduated tokens have locked LP: the LP burn is displayed with the transaction hash. Verifiable. Permanent. Post-graduation tokens cannot be rugged by the creator removing liquidity.

Bonding curve phase is inherently safer than presales: users buy at market price against the curve. There is no pre-sale allocation that the creator dumps from. The creator has no tokens at creation time — they would have to buy from the same curve as everyone else.

Seal-gated content is creator-configured: the platform does not control what creators put behind the paywall. This is disclosed clearly. Creators are responsible for their gated content. The platform provides the tooling, not the editorial guarantee.

## Analytics and Data

The platform should be a data destination, not just a trading UI. The goal is that when a SUI trader wants to understand what's happening in the meme market, they open SuiPump, not a third-party analytics site.

Token-level analytics: full OHLCV history from creation, volume by time period, holder growth curve, buy/sell ratio over time, average trade size, largest single trades, creator wallet activity.

Market-level analytics: total volume across all tokens per day, total tokens created per day, graduation rate (what percentage of tokens graduate), average time to graduation, total SUI locked in bonding curves right now.

Creator analytics (creator-only, private): total fees earned to date, fees per token, which tokens are generating the most passive income, projected earnings based on current volume.

Leaderboards: top creators by graduation rate, top creators by total volume, top traders by realized profit (on-chain verifiable), top tokens by all-time volume.

These analytics make the platform sticky for users who are not actively trading. They come to browse data, stay to trade.

## Revenue Model

The platform generates revenue from:

Creation fees: 0.5 SUI per token. At 500 tokens per day (modest scale), this is 250 SUI/day.

Trading fees during bonding curve: 0.8% platform share of all volume. Bonding curve volume tends to be intense and concentrated in the first hours after launch.

Graduation fee: 1.5 SUI flat fee when a token graduates. Graduation is the platform's highest-value moment — the fee is appropriate.

Post-graduation LP fees: the platform's graduation contract deposits LP on behalf of the creator and takes a portion of the Cetus LP fees earned. The LP is burned (no one can withdraw principal), but fee income from the pool continues to flow to the platform treasury.

API key subscriptions: Pro and Agent tier API keys are monthly subscriptions. Bot builders pay for reliable, high-rate access.

The platform does not take a share of creator fees or insert itself between creator and community financially beyond the explicit fee structure. Creators know exactly what they pay. Traders know exactly what they pay. Trust through transparency.

Being clear about what the platform is not is as important as the vision.
